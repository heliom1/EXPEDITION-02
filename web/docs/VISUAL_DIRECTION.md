# Visual Direction — derived from the master-plan reference images

These are concept references, not assets. The game should match their composition, palette and UI layout within what a browser can render.

## Surface environment (Ref 1 top, Ref 2 hero, Ref 3 top)
- **Sky:** pale salmon/peach near the horizon grading to dusty tan overhead; small, washed-out white sun with a soft halo, low in the sky (golden-hour feel). No blue sky.
- **Haze:** strong warm aerial perspective — distant mesas fade to pale orange-pink; mid-ground stays saturated.
- **Terrain forms:** broad flat basins with scattered rocks, layered **mesas/escarpments with horizontal strata**, rounded distant mountains, shallow craters, dry erosion channels.
- **Ground:** fine rust regolith with dark basalt boulders; rock density clustered (dense at cliff bases, sparse in basins), never uniform.
- **Palette:** iron-oxide red `#9c3d1f`, burnt orange `#c4622d`, ochre `#c98b4a`, dusty brown `#6e3b22`, basalt charcoal `#2b211d`, sky haze `#e7b48f`.
- **Dust storms (Ref 1 "Atmosphere"):** wall of tan dust, visibility drop, orange-brown fog.

## Outpost (Ref 1 "Base & Facilities", Ref 2 hero/bottom-left)
- Cluster of white/grey **cylindrical habitat modules** on legs, connected by tunnels.
- One large **geodesic/greenhouse dome** (farm), a tall **lattice comms/antenna tower**, a **satellite dish**, solar-panel row, cargo crates, an external staircase to an entry hatch.
- Warm interior lights, orange accent stripes.
- **Placeholder only** until the user's outpost model is supplied.

## Rover (Ref 1 "Rover Controls")
- Six-wheel, rocker-bogie style, white/silver body with orange accents, roof light bar, high ground clearance; kicks up dust.
- **Placeholder only** until the user's rover model is supplied.

## Drilling machine (Ref 1/Ref 2)
- Tall lattice derrick on a tracked yellow/rust chassis with a pipe rack; "Drilling in Progress…" progress bar UI.
- **Placeholder only** until supplied.

## Cave (Ref 1 "Cave Exploration", Ref 2, Ref 3 bottom)
- Wide, irregular **arched entrance** from a cliff face, bright desert visible behind the player.
- Interior: rough dark red-brown rock, debris floor, low irregular ceiling, darkness falling off quickly.
- Deep chamber: **cool blue-cyan ice/crystal cluster** as the discovery point, with a faint shaft of light from a ceiling opening and a shallow reflective puddle. Cool light is restrained; the rock stays warm.

## HUD (Ref 1 top panel)
- **Top-left:** "Main Objective" card — diamond icon, title, one-line description.
- **Top-centre:** compass strip (W · NW · N · NE · E).
- **Top-right:** circular minimap with player arrow and objective marker, distance below (e.g. "1.2 km").
- **Bottom-left:** Oxygen (blue), Energy (green), Health (blue/white) bars with percentages.
- **Bottom-centre:** tool hotbar (scanner, sample kit, medkit, canister, battery).
- **Bottom-right:** "[E] Interact" prompt.
- Style: dark graphite translucent panels, thin borders, orange/amber accents, clean sans-serif.

## PDA (Ref 1 bottom-centre)
- Left nav: Map, Missions, Logs, Inventory, Settings.
- Centre: dark topographic satellite map with markers.
- Right: Current Mission title, description, rewards.

## Main menu (Ref 2 bottom-right)
- "CINDERFALL 2" wordmark (white, the "2" in orange), Mars horizon art behind.
- Left list: Continue (highlighted orange), New Game, Settings, Controls, Credits, Quit.
- Tagline bottom-right: "The Red Planet Awaits…"
