2043. Mars. The year is 2043. 10 years have passed since humanity first set foot on the Red Planet. What was once considered an impossible dream has become the beginning of a new era of exploration. Over the past decade, advancements in space technology have transformed what humanity can achieve beyond Earth. New discoveries have expanded our understanding of Mars. Research has pushed the boundaries of human survival, bringing us closer to establishing a permanent presence on another world. NASA has developed advanced systems to support life on the Martian surface. From life support and resource recycling technologies, to sustainable food production and advanced exploration equipment, each development has brought humanity one step closer to calling Mars home. And with every advancement came a new generation of explorers. Astronauts trained not only to survive, but to operate, maintain, and expand the systems that make life on Mars possible. You are one of them! Selected for the second human expedition to Mars, you have been assigned to continue the work of the first crew. Your responsibilities are clear: maintain the existing facilities, operate exploration rovers, conduct research, and document everything you discover. Your mission is to help secure humanity's future on the Red Planet. But a decade of progress does not mean every question has been answered. Some areas remain unexplored. Some research remains unfinished. And the work of the first expedition has left behind more than scientific achievements. There is still much to discover. And now, it is your turn. Your journey begins on Mars.
